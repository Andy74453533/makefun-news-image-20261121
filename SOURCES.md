## Lead: Mamdani unveils plan to close Rikers Island by 2032

- BBC (21 Nov 2026) — New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani unveils a plan
  to close Rikers Island by 2032, replacing it with four smaller jails
  in Brooklyn, the Bronx, Queens and Manhattan, and cutting the inmate
  population from about 6,500 to 4,100. First jail in Brooklyn by 2029,
  two more in the Bronx and Queens by 2031, the fourth in Manhattan
  by 2032.

## EU and Indonesia sign landmark trade deal

- Further Asia / ANTARA (21 Nov 2026) — the Indonesia–EU Comprehensive
  Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) is signed in Brussels on
  21 November 2026, liberalising about 98% of bilateral trade worth
  roughly $30bn a year; 90.4% of Indonesian product lines get zero
  tariffs at entry into force. The EU Council authorised signature on
  18 November 2026 (Consilium press release).

## NATO chief Rutte visits Warsaw

- AP / Reuters via multiple outlets (21 Nov 2026) — NATO Secretary
  General Mark Rutte visits Warsaw on Saturday 21 November, as Poland
  seeks more Eastern Sentry forces and air defences after Russian
  missiles landed in Poland on Friday 20 November. Tusk called the
  incursion a deliberate provocation.

## Florida floods push north as river hits record

- Associated Press / National Weather Service (21 Nov 2026) — the
  St. Johns River near Cocoa reaches 18.8 ft, above its 1953 record of
  17.55 ft, as historic flooding shifts further north; flood watch in
  place through Saturday afternoon, with nearly 100 high-water rescues
  in Brevard County.

## Iraq opens security HQ in Jurf al-Sakhar

- Reuters (20–21 Nov 2026) — Iraqi security forces deployed in
  Jurf al-Sakhar on Friday under a deal with Kataib Hezbollah; the
  security headquarters opens on Saturday 21 November to oversee the
  handover, with the town closed to the group for about 12 years.
